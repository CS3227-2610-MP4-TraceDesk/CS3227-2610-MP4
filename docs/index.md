# TraceDesk

**TraceDesk helps software teams report bugs, investigate them and verify proposed fixes.** Reporters document the issue, Developers investigate and propose a resolution, and QA reviewers verify the result. AI suggestions are advisory and require human review.

- Read the [User Guide](UserGuide.md) for role workflows and local access.
- Read the [Developer Guide](DeveloperGuide.md) for setup, architecture, security, testing and the development process.
- Read [Reflections](Reflections.md) for recorded lessons and evidence.
